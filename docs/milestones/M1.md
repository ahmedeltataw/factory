- `CHK-M1-03-d`: `scripts/check-evidence` fails on a record with `result: not-run` or missing fields; passes on a valid one. (negative test)
- `CHK-M1-03-e`: `scripts/check-scope` fails when a diff touches `tests/protected/**`, `docs/**`, `schemas/**` or `.factory/**`. (negative test)
