Source of truth: design manual `08-lead-brain-and-cards.md`, `09-security.md`, `10-factory-repo-and-build-plan.md` §7 (stays local, never pushed).

## Goal

Give the Supervisor a deterministic, stateless Lead Brain contract: versioned JSON Schemas (Draft 2020-12) and Zod validators for all 7 core call types, a deterministic post-model semantic and policy veto engine, 3-layer role cards with a byte-exact stable prompt prefix and untrusted-evidence isolation, a replayable stateless runner with bounded recovery (1 deterministic repair → 1 cross-family retry → safe escalation) and crash-safe pending-row recovery, a 20-fixture evaluation suite with adversarial mutations, CLI/command/status observability, and a Windows fresh-clone acceptance script.

Scope boundaries and exemptions:
- Maintenance mode (`admit` call type) is **M10**, not M8.
- Real LLM CLI/HTTP execution adapters stay out of M8 (`FakeBrainPort` exercises the full runner, repair, cross-family retry, outage, and replay paths deterministically; live provider wiring belongs to M9/M11).
- `supervisor/src/core/` remains strictly pure (`./` and `node:crypto` only, enforced by `scripts/check-forbidden-imports.mjs`). Lead Brain modules live under `supervisor/src/brain/` (same boundary as `supervisor/src/sources/github/`: may use `zod`, `node:*`, and `../app/*` or `../core/*`, MUST have zero DSH/Cordis/Feishu imports checked by `check-forbidden-imports.mjs` and zero LLM vendor SDK imports).
- Stryker mutation ≥ 80% applies to all new logic modules (`schemas.ts`, `policy-veto.ts`, `cards.ts`, `prompt-prefix.ts`, `triggers.ts`, `runner.ts`) and updated core modules (`store.ts`, `apply-commands.ts`, `status.ts`). Explicit recorded exemptions (Muse `#34-10`): `supervisor/src/brain/ports.ts` (type-only interfaces), `supervisor/src/brain/fake-brain.ts` (test double), `supervisor/src/brain/eval-fixtures.ts` (static fixture data), and `supervisor/src/cli/main.ts` (child-process CLI, unchanged from M6/M7).

- Common envelope required on every Lead Brain response (`08` §2):

- Per-call-type fields (`08` §2–§3):

### Deterministic semantic & policy veto engine (`supervisor/src/brain/policy-veto.ts`)

Pure function `evaluatePolicyVetoes(callType, parsedOutput, context?)` runs deterministically **after** schema validation and is always authoritative over model-supplied `policy_check`:
- Pure and stateless: **never consults `brain_overrides`** (Muse `#34-12`); a Designer override is an audit record (`08` §7) and never retroactively un-vetoes a model output inside the runner.
- Computes `allowed = vetoes.length === 0` and deduplicates + lexicographically sorts `vetoes`.
- **Path normalization & case handling (Muse `#34-4`):** normalize all `\` to `/`, strip repeated leading `./` segments, reject any path containing a `..` segment, null byte, leading `/`, Windows drive prefix (`^[a-z]:`), or UNC prefix (`^//`) as `forbidden-path`, then compare **case-insensitively (lowercased)** against default forbidden prefixes (`tests/protected/`, `tests/qa/`, `schemas/`, `.factory/`, `licenses/`) and exact files (`source_manifest.json`, `notice`, `license`), plus any `context.forbiddenPaths` (similarly normalized and lowercased).
- **Common provenance rule (Muse `#34-8`):** `missing-evidence` whenever `parsedOutput.status === 'ok'` and `parsedOutput.provenance.length === 0`.
- Frozen veto codes:
  - Common: `forbidden-path`, `missing-evidence`.
  - `plan`: `task-missing-owner` (blank `owner`), `task-missing-dod` (empty `done[]` or blank item), `dependency-without-adr` (non-empty `new_dependencies` when `adrs` is empty), `invented-destination` (non-empty `invented_destinations` or target outside `context.allowedDestinations`), `task-graph-invalid` (duplicate `task_graph[].id`, dangling `deps[]` not in `task_graph` or `context.existingTaskIds`, or any dependency cycle — Muse `#34-7`), `forbidden-path`.
  - `answer`: `forbidden-path` (any `request_edit` targeting a forbidden path), `waive-acceptance-criteria` (any action with `kind === 'waive_criterion'`).
  - `unblock`: `s0-s1-waiver` (`waived_severities` includes `S0` or `S1`), `destructive-action` (`destructive === true`), `policy-override` (`policy_override === true`), `forbidden-path` (`target_paths` or `split_tasks[].scope` hits forbidden path), `retry-without-cause-or-change` (`action === 'retry_with_change'` with blank `root_cause` or blank `retry_change`), `insufficient-evidence-without-single-question` (`status === 'insufficient_evidence'` or `action === 'ask_question'` with `question === null`, blank/whitespace-only `question`, or more than one `?` character `(question.match(/\?/g) ?? []).length > 1` — Muse `#34-9`).
  - `triage`: `downgrade-without-evidence` (bug `severity` lowered relative to `original_severity`, where `S0 > S1 > S2 > S3 > S4`, with empty `evidence_refs`), `s0-s1-waiver` (any waiver for `S0` or `S1`), `qa-test-edit` (non-empty `qa_test_edits` or forbidden path).
  - `arbitrate`: `approve-against-adr` (`ruling === 'approve'` with `violates_adr === true`), `high-risk-without-lead-review` (`ruling === 'approve'` with `(risk ?? context?.risk) === 'high'` and `lead_reviewed !== true`).
  - `change-impact`: `silent-contract-break` (`breaks_contract === true` with `adr_required !== true` or `decision === 'proceed'` without an `adr`), `dependency-without-adr` (non-empty `new_dependencies` without `adr_required === true` or `adr`), `forbidden-path`.
  - `milestone-review`: `complete-with-open-s0-s1` (`complete === true` with `(open_s0_s1_count ?? context?.openS0S1Count ?? 0) > 0`), `complete-with-missing-evidence` (`complete === true` with non-empty `missing_evidence` or non-empty `context?.missingEvidence`).

### Role cards (3 layers) & stable prompt prefix (`cards/core/`, `supervisor/src/brain/cards.ts`, `supervisor/src/brain/prompt-prefix.ts`)

- 7 CORE cards under `cards/core/`: `builder-ui.md`, `builder-logic.md`, `reviewer.md`, `qa.md`, `ops.md`, `writer.md`, `lead.md`.
- Every card starts with a structured frontmatter block validated against `schemas/cards/card.schema.json`:
  - `card_id`, `role`, `layer` (`'core' | 'stack' | 'project'`), `card_version` (strict semver `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH`), `capabilities` (non-empty string array for core), `forbidden_paths` (string array).
- Hard rules enforced by `cards.ts`:
  - CORE card total line count **≤ 80 lines** (LF-normalized).
  - Stack and project addenda NEVER hold factory-rule overrides (reject forbidden directives like `FACTORY RULES:`, `POLICY OVERRIDE:`, or `override_forbidden_paths` in frontmatter/body).
- Stable prompt prefix (`buildBrainPrompt`):
  - Exact layer order: `core` → `stack` (optional) → `project` (optional) → `call_instructions` (static template per `call_type`) → `task_bundle` → `untrusted_evidence`.
  - Line endings normalized to `\n` before hashing so `prefixHash` is byte-identical across Windows and Linux.
  - `systemPrefix` contains ONLY `core → stack → project → call_instructions`. `prefixHash = sha256(systemPrefix)`.
  - `userBundle` contains structured task metadata + an `UNTRUSTED_EVIDENCE` section where every evidence item is serialized with `{ id, source, trust: 'untrusted' | 'verified', sha256 }` and its text is delimiter-escaped (neutralizing injected delimiter tokens and stripping ASCII control chars except `\n`/`\t`).
  - `inputHash = sha256(systemPrefix + '\n---\n' + userBundle)`.
  - Two calls sharing `(core, stack, project, call_type)` with different tasks or untrusted evidence MUST produce identical `systemPrefix` bytes and `prefixHash`.

### Stateless Brain Runner, triggers, bounded recovery & crash recovery (`supervisor/src/brain/ports.ts`, `triggers.ts`, `runner.ts`, `fake-brain.ts`)

- `TRIGGER_RULES_VERSION = 1`. `deriveBrainIdempotencyKey({ projectId, callType, triggerKind, triggerVersion, targetKey })` computes a deterministic SHA-256 hex key.
- `shouldTriggerUnblock({ sameCauseFailures, blockedMs, failureThreshold = 3, blockedThresholdMs })` deterministically decides whether an `unblock` call is justified.
- `runBrainCall(db, leaseHandle, port, request)`:
  1. **Idempotent replay & orphaned `pending` recovery (Muse `#34-2`):**
     - Looks up `brain_calls` by `(project_id, idempotency_key)`.
     - If a **terminal** row exists (`status !== 'pending'`), returns that stored replay record immediately with zero `port.invoke` calls.
     - If an **orphaned `pending`** row exists (from a prior crash or lease loss mid-call), loads its existing `brain_call_attempts` (ordered by `attempt_no`) and resumes on that same `call_id` (NEVER inserting a duplicate `brain_calls` row):
       - If an existing attempt already has `outcome === 'valid'` or `'vetoed'`, finalizes `brain_calls` directly from that attempt's stored `raw_output` without re-invoking `port`.
       - If no attempts exist yet (`count === 0`), runs from step 3 (`primary`, `attempt_no = 1`).
       - If attempt 1 (`primary`) exists with `invalid_output` and no `repair` attempt exists yet, runs deterministic repair (`attempt_no = 2`) then optional cross-family retry (`attempt_no = 3`).
       - If attempt 1 (`primary`) exists with `provider_error`, or a `repair` attempt already failed, and no `cross_family_retry` exists yet, runs optional cross-family retry (`attempt_no = nextAttemptNo <= 3`) or finalizes `escalated` if no `retryModel` is configured.
       - If a `cross_family_retry` already exists (or `attempt_no >= 3`), finalizes `brain_calls` as `escalated` (`brain-unavailable` if last attempt was `provider_error`, else `brain-recovery-exhausted`) without re-invoking `port`.
  2. Otherwise inserts `brain_calls` (`status = 'pending'`) with `input_hash`, `prefix_hash`, `card_versions_json`, `memory_query_ids_json`.
  3. **Attempt 1 (`stage: 'primary'`):** invokes `port.invoke(primaryModel, prompt)` and persists `brain_call_attempts` with `raw_output`, `output_hash`, provider/model/family/cli_version, timestamps.
  4. **On provider error/unavailable:** records `outcome = 'provider_error'`. If `retryModel` is configured (must satisfy `retryModel.family !== primaryModel.family`, else refused with `same-family-retry-forbidden`), runs **1 cross-family retry** (`stage: 'cross_family_retry'`). If still unavailable or no `retryModel` is configured, finalizes `brain_calls` with `status = 'escalated'`, `escalation_reason = 'brain-unavailable'`.
  5. **On invalid JSON or schema error on primary (Muse `#34-1`):** records `outcome = 'invalid_output'`.
     - **Repair applies at most once, to the primary attempt's output only:** runs `repairJsonOutput` (strips outer Markdown fences, extracts single balanced top-level `{...}` object, removes trailing commas — never invents missing fields or modifies semantic values).
     - If repaired output parses and passes schema: persists attempt 2 (`stage: 'repair'`, `outcome: 'valid'` or `'vetoed'`) and proceeds to step 6.
     - If repair fails schema: persists attempt 2 (`stage: 'repair'`, `outcome: 'invalid_output'`), then if `retryModel` is configured (strictly enforcing `retryModel.family !== primaryModel.family`), invokes `retryModel` as attempt 3 (`stage: 'cross_family_retry'`). **A failed cross-family retry output is NEVER repaired** — it immediately finalizes `status = 'escalated'`, `escalation_reason = 'brain-recovery-exhausted'`, `final_output_json = null` (never producing a Designer product question). If no `retryModel` is configured, finalizes immediately as `status = 'escalated'`, `escalation_reason = 'brain-recovery-exhausted'`.
  6. **On schema-valid output (from primary, repair, or cross-family retry):** runs `evaluatePolicyVetoes(callType, parsed, vetoContext)`.
     - If `vetoes.length > 0`: finalizes `brain_calls` with `status = 'vetoed'`, `vetoes_json`, `final_output_json` (with enforced `policy_check: { allowed: false, vetoes }`), `final_output_hash`.
     - Otherwise: finalizes `brain_calls` with `status` (`ok` / `insufficient_evidence` / `escalated` with `escalation_reason = 'model-escalated'` when `output.status === 'escalate'`), `final_output_json`, `final_output_hash`.
  - Every DB write goes through `fencedWrite(db, leaseHandle, ...)` when `leaseHandle` is supplied.
  - Hard invariant: total `brain_call_attempts` per `call_id` is ALWAYS `<= 3`.

### Commands, CLI & Status (`commands.ts`, `apply-commands.ts`, `status.ts`, `cli/main.ts`)

- Append-only commands (CLI queues to `commands` only; Supervisor applies):
  - `brain.override`: `{ author, reason, scope, callId?, expiresAt?, followupAdr? }` → inserts into `brain_overrides`.
    - **Frozen `scope` grammar (Muse `#34-6`):** non-empty array of 1..20 trimmed non-empty strings, each 1..128 chars matching `/^[a-zA-Z0-9._:/-]+$/` with no `..` segment (deduplicated and sorted when stored in `scope_json`). CLI `--scope` parses a comma-separated list into this array.
    - Frozen rejection reasons: `missing-author`, `missing-reason`, `invalid-scope`, `unknown-call-id`, `invalid-expiry`.
  - `brain.request`: `{ callType, idempotencyKey, triggerKind, taskId?, bundleJson }` → validates payload and marks command `applied` in `commands` ONLY (does **not** pre-insert a `pending` row in `brain_calls`; `runBrainCall` owns `brain_calls` creation and deduplicates by `(project_id, idempotency_key)`, Muse `#34-5`).
    - Frozen rejection reasons: `unknown-call-type`, `missing-key`, `missing-trigger`, `unknown-task`, `invalid-bundle`.
- Pure/read-only CLI subcommands (never write state tables directly):
  - `factory brain validate --type <callType> --file <path> [--json]` — validates JSON against schema + deterministic policy vetoes (exit 0 allowed, exit 1 invalid/vetoed).
  - `factory brain prompt --type <callType> --card <role> [--stack <path>] [--project <path>] [--bundle <path>] [--json]` — renders stable prompt prefix and SHA-256 hashes.
  - `factory brain override --state-dir <dir> --author <name> --reason <text> --scope <csv> [--call-id <id>] [--expires-at <iso>] [--adr <id>] [--json]` — queues `brain.override` in `commands`.
- Status additions (`status.ts`, `cli/main.ts`):
  - `schemaVersion`: `10`.
  - `brain`: `null` when both `brain_calls` and `brain_overrides` are empty; otherwise `{ totalCalls, byStatus, byType, invalidOutputCount, invalidOutputRate, repairSuccessCount, crossFamilyRetryCount, vetoedCount, escalatedCount, overrideCount, recent }` (last 10 calls ordered newest-first).
  - Arabic output shows exact factual metrics only.
